About

A bit about me

I'm Lynx. This is my third website, the first was a GitHub Pages site (now folded into Portfolio). The second website was a standalone website to my alternate history project i'm making, but i decided to merge it into this website (The Old World Eternal). I build things on the web when I can, and I spend a lot of the rest of the time reading, playing games and writing history that didn't quite happen.

Interests

I love programming — messing with code, static sites, and small tools that do one thing well. I'm also a massive Chinese history nerd: dynasties, reforms, rebellions, the long arcs that connect the past to now. That mix of systems and stories is a big part of why I started The Old World Eternal.
