Timeline

Year-by-year record of the timeline, from the fall of the Kingdom of Jerusalem in 1178 to the present day. Each entry links to a fuller write-up on Figures, Events, or Realms.

Timeline Figures Events Realms
1174

Baldwin IV crowned in Jerusalem

Baldwin IV takes the throne of a Crusader kingdom already strained by debt, factionalism, and his own declining health — the fragile status quo that the timeline diverges from.

1178

The Jasmine Revolt

Sabira bint Yusef leads the coalition that deposes Baldwin IV and takes Jerusalem, ending centralized Crusader rule and founding what will become the Sabirid Dynasty. This is the true point of divergence.

1178–1213

Reign of Sabira bint Yusef

The Sabirid Dynasty consolidates power in the Levant, rebuilding trade and administration on the ruins of the old Crusader kingdom.

Late 12th c. – 1783

The Age of Inspiration

A long era of intellectual, cultural, and scientific flourishing across the Middle East and beyond, laying the foundations of the later Jasmine Empire.

1636

Qing Dynasty established

A Manchu-led dynasty takes power in China, ruling until its authority collapses under the Tianming Rebellion.

Late 17th c.

Tianming Rebellion

A major anti-Qing uprising — the “Disorder of the Heavenly Mandate” — breaks centralized Qing authority in China.

1649

Fu Dynasty rises; Kingdom of Ireland founded

In China, the Fu Dynasty emerges from Qing collapse. In the same year, Irish forces expel English rule and establish the Kingdom of Ireland.

1707

Empire of Iberia formed

Spain and Portugal are joined under a single dual monarchy, ruling both home territory and their overseas colonies.

1799

Empire of Gaul founded

The Bourbon monarchy collapses in France; a new republican-imperial system rises in its place, styling itself heir to Rome.

1820

Britannic Commonwealth declared

The English monarchy falls in the Industrial Riots. A republican Commonwealth inherits Britain's industrial empire.

1847–1906

Amina III, the Physician-Empress

Amina III rules the Jasmine Empire from 1878, remembered for her patronage of medicine and science.

1857

Norse Union of Odin unified

Denmark, Norway, and Sweden are joined under a neo-pagan pan-Scandinavian monarchy following the Norse National Awakening.

1874–1882

First Great Asian War

The Eastern Concord and the Northern League fight the deadliest conflict of the 19th century, closing the Age of Inspiration and opening the Age of Steel.

1889

Preußischer Volksbund declared

A republican Prussian People's Commonwealth rises in Central Europe.

1891–1893

The Balkan War and the Fall of Prussia

A short, destructive war between Prussia and the Jasmine Empire ends in Prussia's collapse as a great power.

1890s

Revolts across the Jasmine Empire

Uprisings and strikes shake the Jasmine Empire in the wake of its costly Balkan War.

1906–1932

Road to the Second Great Asian War

Decades of mounting tension between the old rivals culminate in a second, larger global conflict.

1932–1956

Second Great Asian War

The Hilāl Bloc and the Iron Pact fight a war that reshapes the globe and sets the stage for the Sabirid state's final decades.

1956–2012

The Eternal Sabirid State

The Jasmine Throne hardens into an authoritarian state built on the doctrine of Eternal Purity, marked by surveillance and repression.
